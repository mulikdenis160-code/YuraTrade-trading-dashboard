name: Signal statistics integrity
description: Rules for trustworthy outcome totals and performance claims in PRIME BOT.
---

PRIME BOT actual performance totals and hit rates must come from verified terminal signal-series outcomes. Keep draws out of the hit-rate denominator, count an overlay sequence as one series, and identify that verified data is local to this browser rather than platform-wide. Never present synthetic performance as historical results; any optional demo must be prominently labeled, separate, static, and excluded from real statistics.

Keep outcome tracking quote-based only. Do not add manual +/− controls or count self-reported results as verified without an explicit scope change.

**Why:** The user chose to keep automated quote checks only.

**How to apply:** When changing the journal, statistics, or performance copy, preserve the verified-outcome rules and clearly state data scope. Keep any demo UI static and distinct, and treat historical results as non-predictive.
