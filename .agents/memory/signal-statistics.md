---
name: Signal statistics integrity
description: Rules for trustworthy outcome totals and performance claims in PRIME BOT.
---

PRIME BOT performance totals and hit rates must come from verified terminal signal-series outcomes. Keep draws out of the hit-rate denominator, count an overlay sequence as one series, and identify that the displayed data is local to this browser rather than platform-wide. Never seed or label synthetic performance as historical results.

**Why:** The requested illustrative starting total and rate were not verified outcomes; presenting them as real performance would mislead users.

**How to apply:** When changing the journal, statistics, or performance copy, preserve the verified-outcome rules and clearly state the data scope. Treat historical results as non-predictive.
